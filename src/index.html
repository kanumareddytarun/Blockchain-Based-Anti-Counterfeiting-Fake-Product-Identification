About Our Project

Our project uses blockchain technology to detect counterfeit products and secure the authenticity of consumer goods. By tracking products along the supply chain, we ensure that the product you receive is genuine.

Every product is tagged with a unique QR code, which can be scanned by consumers to verify its authenticity using our platform. Blockchain ensures transparency and immutability, making it nearly impossible to counterfeit products.

Key Features

Blockchain Security

Leverage the immutability and transparency of blockchain to secure the supply chain and prevent counterfeiting.

QR Code Authentication

Each product is assigned a unique QR code that consumers can scan to verify its authenticity in real-time.

Real-time Tracking

Track your products through the entire supply chain, ensuring their safety from manufacturer to consumer.

How It Works

  • Add Product: The manufacturer adds a new product to the system, and this is securely recorded on the blockchain, ensuring it becomes part of the traceable supply chain.
  • Add Seller: The manufacturer can associate specific sellers who will handle their products, ensuring that only authorized sellers distribute their products.
  • Sell Product to Seller: The sale of a product from the manufacturer to a seller is recorded, allowing the product's journey to be tracked and authenticated on the blockchain.
  • Query Seller: Manufacturers can query sellers to track where their products are currently being sold, enabling inventory tracking and fraud prevention.

  • Sell Product to Consumer: Sellers can list products for consumers and facilitate their sales, ensuring that the entire transaction is recorded and traceable back to the manufacturer.
  • Products for Sale: Sellers can manage their inventory, viewing the products they have in stock and keeping track of which products are currently available for consumers to purchase.

  • Consumer Purchase History: Consumers can easily access their purchase history, helping them confirm the authenticity and traceability of the products they have bought.
  • Product Verification: By scanning a QR code or barcode provided with the product, consumers can verify the product’s authenticity and view its journey from manufacturer to seller, confirming it is genuine.
